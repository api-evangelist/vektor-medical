# Vektor Medical

> Vektor Medical is a San Diego based medical technology company. Its vMap system is an

> chambers, non-invasively, in about a minute.

## Product

- [vMap](https://www.vektormedical.com/vmap): Non-invasive four-chamber arrhythmia source
  mapping from 12-lead ECG. Inputs 12-lead ECG data; outputs beat-by-beat 2D and 3D
  cardiac maps. Used for triage, procedural planning and ablation guidance across focal
  and fibrillation arrhythmias and atrial and ventricular pacing.
